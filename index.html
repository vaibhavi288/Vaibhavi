The Theory

What is Double Integration?

Double integration is used to integrate functions with two variables. It helps calculate area and volume.

Area Calculation

When the integrand is 1, the double integral calculates area of the region.

Volume Calculation

Double integration gives volume under a surface z = f(x,y).

Key Formulas

∬ f(x,y) dA
A = ∬ 1 dA
V = ∬ f(x,y)dA

Worked Example

Question: Evaluate ∬(x+2y)dA over R = [0,2] × [0,1]

Step 1: Set up the integral

∫₀¹ ∫₀² (x + 2y) dx dy

Step 2: Solve inner integral

∫₀² (x + 2y) dx = [x²/2 + 2yx]₀²

= 2 + 4y

Step 3: Solve outer integral

∫₀¹ (2 + 4y)dy

= [2y + 2y²]₀¹

= 2 + 2

Final Answer: 4
